## Capabilities

**Public safety and law enforcement.** Fraud investigation, stolen-vehicle coordination, OSINT and cold-case intelligence.

**Sovereign AI and retrieval.** Retrieval-augmented analysis that runs on-premises or at the edge. No data is sent to third-party model providers.

**Fraud and financial crime.** Plate, title, check, and identity fraud, with cross-agency intelligence sharing.

**Emergency management.** County-deployable situational awareness and operations mapping.

**Cybersecurity and assurance.** Deepfake and synthetic-media detection, brand and clone-site protection, and framework-based assessment against NIST CSF, 800-171, CMMC, CIS, and CISA CPG.
